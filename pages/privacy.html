Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what mysabak collects when you ride, send a parcel, order food, drive or sell with us in Cebu, why we need it, and the choices you have. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, mobile number and email.
  • Your trips and orders: pickup and drop-off points, the route, the time, the vehicle, the fare, and what you ordered from a shop.
  • Your location: only when you allow it, to set your pickup and to show your driver where you are.
  • Payments: how you chose to pay (cash, GCash or online banking) and the amount. GCash and banks process the payment; we don’t store your card or account numbers.
  • Tips you give mysabak: the amount and when.
  • Drivers: your vehicle details, your driver’s licence and vehicle registration (OR/CR), and your trips and earnings.
  • Shops: your shop’s name, address, hours, menu and orders.

How we use it

  • To match you with a driver, show the route and the fare, and let you follow your trip or order.
  • To pay drivers and shops what they earned.
  • To check that drivers and shops are who they say they are.
  • To help when something goes wrong with a trip, an order or a payment.
  • To keep mysabak safe and to meet our legal duties.

We don’t sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Your driver or shop: what they need for your trip or order, such as your pickup point and first name.
  • Maps: addresses and map positions go to our map provider, TomTom, to find places and routes.
  • Payments: GCash or your bank, when you pay that way.
  • The authorities: only when the law requires it.

On your phone

The mysabak app keeps some things on your device so it opens fast and works with a weak signal, such as your recent places and your cart. You can clear them by signing out or by clearing your browser’s site data.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you use mysabak, and trip, order and payment records for as long as the law requires. When you close your account, we delete or anonymise the rest.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can object to how we use it. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and tell you in the app when the change matters to you.
