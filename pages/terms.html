Terms of service

Last updated 18 November 2026

These terms cover your use of mysabak in Cebu: booking rides and moto rides, sending parcels, ordering food from shops, and, if you join us, driving or selling with mysabak. By using mysabak you agree to them.

Using mysabak

  • Give us true details and keep your account to yourself.
  • Treat drivers, shop staff and other riders with respect.
  • Don’t send anything illegal or dangerous as a parcel.

Fares and payment

  • The fare is the base fare plus distance, and you see it before you book.
  • There are no booking fees.
  • You can pay with cash, GCash or online banking.
  • Your driver keeps the whole fare. If you choose to add an extra for mysabak, it goes to mysabak and never comes out of the driver’s fare.

For drivers

  • You drive as an independent driver, when you choose to.
  • Your licence, vehicle registration (OR/CR) and vehicle must be valid, and you must follow traffic laws.
  • You keep 100% of every fare. mysabak takes no commission and charges you no booking fees.

For shops

  • Keep your menu, prices and opening hours accurate.
  • Prepare orders safely and on time, and follow food-safety rules.

Closing an account

You can stop using mysabak at any time. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms or puts others at risk.

The law
